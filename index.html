how eski works

comics, scored and voiced.

the soundtrack follows the page music is mapped to page ranges, so turning a page changes the score.
dialogue is voiced each character is performed by a voice actor. sound effects sit alongside them.
you pick the mix swap in a different cast or a different soundtrack, or read it silent.
anyone can contribute voice a character, score a comic, or publish your own.
