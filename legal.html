Takedowns

eski hosts comics, music and voice recordings uploaded by the people who made them. If something here is yours and should not be, it will be removed.

Reporting something

Email agent@eski.lol — set this up with:

  1. What was copied — enough to identify the work, or a list if there are several.
  2. Where it is on eski — a link to the comic, the part, or the page.
  3. How to reach you: name, address, telephone, email.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and — under penalty of perjury — that you are the owner or authorised to act for them.
  6. Your signature, physical or electronic.

What happens next

  • The material is removed or disabled promptly.
  • Whoever uploaded it is told it was removed, and why.
  • They may send a counter-notice. If they do, you will be sent a copy.
  • If nobody files a court action within the statutory window, the material may be restored.
  • Accounts that repeatedly infringe are terminated.

Terms

What you keep

Your work stays yours. Publishing on eski grants only what is needed to run the site: to store your files, and to show and play them to readers.

Contributions

A voiceover or a score you record for someone else's comic is a Part, and it stays yours too. Parts are credited by name, and layered onto a comic only when a reader chooses them.

You may withdraw a part at any time. The comic keeps working without it — the character simply goes back to being unvoiced.

Decide and state: may an author reuse a contributed part outside eski, and is AI-generated voice allowed and must it be labelled?

What you may not upload

  • Work you do not have the rights to.
  • Anyone's voice recorded without their knowledge or against their wishes.
  • Anything unlawful.

Privacy

What is stored

  • Your account — whatever your sign-in provider tells us, which is your email and display name.
  • What you published — comics, parts, and the files behind them.
  • Your shelf — which comics you saved and the page you reached.
  • Tags and kudos you added, attributed to you.

What is not

There are no advertising trackers and no analytics on eski.
