programme 14 · week of 3 august 2026

comics you can hear

seven comics this week. every page turn moves the score, every line is performed by a real person, and you choose who performs it. three of the seven are still missing a voice.

blame! master edition

tsutomu nihei · 368 pages · 12 chapters

nihei lets the architecture do the work: corridors that run for chapters, stairs that go nowhere, and almost nobody talking. the score carries it, which is exactly the case eski was built for. one part, dhomochevsky, is still open.

no. 01added monday needs one voice — dhomochevsky, 9 lines 212 kudos

falling in love on the path to hell

duggan & brown · 152 pages · 12 chapters

asami dies an onna-musha and macraith dies a gunslinger, a world and three centuries apart, and they wake in the same purgatory. duggan writes them talking their way through it, which makes it the most dialogue-heavy thing on the shelf. three mixes exist; start with the first.

no. 02added monday fully cast · 3 mixes 1,104 kudos

barbaric: axe to grind

moreci, gooden, duke & campbell· 128 pages

a sentient weapon that will not shut up, which turns out to be the ideal test case for the whole platform: axe is voiced separately from everyone it talks over, so the mix you pick genuinely changes the joke. four submitted casts so far and no two are alike.

no. 03leaving sunday 4 casts submitted 640 kudos
