How to read this

The visual companion to docs/CANON.md. Screens are the live mockup, embedded (interact with them). Dialogs, menus and modals are authored panels — modals sit on a darkened backdrop, no drop shadow (the rule); menus float flat. Every panel names what it is, its trigger, and its database binding. Toggle Dark to check both themes. Radius is 3px everywhere; round is only avatars and presence dots; closes are square. “Desktop” means the web app in a wide browser. The embedded screens now say server and friends (renamed from group/follows).

① Member colours — the one hue in the UI, proposed at 30

Chrome is black/white/grey; the only colour is a member’s per-server identity hue, shown only inside that server and never on a public profile or the Feed. Widened from 6 to 30 perceptually-even hues (OKLCH, so neighbours are equally distinguishable) (--m1 … --m30), each a light + dark pair; join_via_invite assigns the next free index and cycles past 30. A generated proposal to critique before it’s recorded in the eski-style skill.

▸ Each swatch: light half · dark half, current-theme hex below. ▸ Chips preview names in each hue. ▸ Critique: separation? too saturated on dark? cap at 24?

② The screens — live, embedded (these are law)

Each is the real mockup at collab-mockup.html#screen. Their own overlays (details pane, lightbox) are reachable by clicking in.
